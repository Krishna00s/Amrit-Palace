# Amrit Palace — Product Vision

## 1. The Vision

Amrit Palace is a real hospitality destination in Lohardaga, Jharkhand — a place where people come to stay, dine, celebrate, meet, gather and create meaningful moments.

This project is about translating that physical experience into a modern digital experience.

The goal is to create a website and hospitality platform that feels like a genuine digital extension of Amrit Palace rather than a conventional hotel website.

The experience should immediately communicate:

> **A Place for Every Occasion.**

A visitor should be able to discover Amrit Palace visually, understand what the property offers, explore its spaces and experiences, plan a stay or event, and eventually interact with the hotel through a connected digital platform.

The website is therefore both:

- a cinematic public-facing brand experience
- the front door to a functional hospitality platform

The result should feel intentional, contemporary, memorable and genuinely useful.

---

# 2. What We Are Building

Amrit Palace will eventually become a connected hospitality system consisting of multiple experiences built around one shared platform.

### Public Website

The public-facing experience introduces Amrit Palace and allows visitors to explore:

- the property
- rooms
- dining
- celebrations
- weddings
- birthdays
- meetings and conferences
- other events and services
- the brand story
- contact and location information

The public website should create desire and confidence before asking the visitor to take action.

---

### Guest Experience

Guests should eventually have their own digital space where they can:

- create an account
- authenticate using their phone and OTP
- view their stay
- manage bookings
- explore and order food
- view order status
- see accumulated charges
- view their bill
- complete checkout and payment
- manage relevant profile information

The guest experience should feel like a natural continuation of the public website rather than a completely unrelated application.

---

### Chef Experience

The kitchen team should have a dedicated operational interface for:

- receiving food orders
- accepting orders
- preparing orders
- marking orders ready
- marking orders delivered
- managing kitchen activity
- recording ingredient consumption
- viewing relevant inventory information

The kitchen experience should prioritize speed, clarity and practical usability.

---

### Admin Experience

The hotel administration should eventually have a central operational interface for:

- bookings
- guests
- food orders
- menu management
- rooms
- services and events
- payments
- inventory
- staff
- reports
- operational insights

The admin experience should turn the platform into a useful management system rather than simply being a collection of dashboards.

---

# 3. The Core Idea

The entire public experience is built around four fundamental ideas:

## STAY

A comfortable place for guests to rest and continue their journey.

Amrit Palace currently offers its actual guest-room experience as a comfortable room with features such as air conditioning, a master bed and other practical guest amenities.

The digital experience should represent the room honestly while presenting it beautifully.

---

## DINE

Food is part of the hospitality experience.

Guests should be able to discover the available menu, understand dishes, see pricing and eventually order food through the platform.

Dining should feel connected to the stay rather than existing as an isolated restaurant feature.

---

## CELEBRATE

Amrit Palace provides a place for people to celebrate meaningful occasions.

This includes experiences such as:

- weddings
- birthdays
- anniversaries
- ceremonies
- private celebrations
- social gatherings

The digital experience should communicate the emotion and possibility of these occasions primarily through strong visual storytelling.

---

## MEET

Amrit Palace is also a place where people come together professionally.

The experience should communicate possibilities such as:

- corporate meetings
- conferences
- business gatherings
- organized events

This creates an important contrast within the brand:

A place can host a joyful family celebration in one moment and a focused professional gathering in another.

That versatility is part of the identity.

---

# 4. The Public Website Experience

The public website should feel like a cinematic journey.

The visitor should not feel as though they are simply scrolling through a collection of hotel sections.

Instead, the page should gradually reveal the world of Amrit Palace.

A possible emotional progression is:

**ARRIVE → DISCOVER → STAY → DINE → CELEBRATE → MEET → CONNECT**

The exact implementation should be determined by the design and engineering process.

The important objective is that every section contributes to the story.

---

# 5. The Brand Film

A major visual element of the landing experience is the cinematic Amrit Palace film.

The current assembled film is approximately 33 seconds long and contains a sequence built from multiple cinematic clips.

The visual journey explores:

- arrival at Amrit Palace
- entering the property
- discovering the interior
- the banquet/event environment
- Indian wedding celebrations
- family celebrations
- children's birthday celebrations
- professional meetings
- the identity of Amrit Palace

The film eventually resolves into a dedicated Amrit Palace brand moment:

**AMRIT PALACE**

**A PLACE FOR EVERY OCCASION**

This film should be treated as an important piece of the website's storytelling system.

It should not simply exist as decorative background footage.

The implementation should make the cinematic footage and the interface feel like two layers of the same experience:

**the film creates emotion; the interface creates clarity and action.**

---

# 6. Visual Philosophy

The visual direction should feel contemporary, cinematic and premium while remaining authentic to the actual Amrit Palace property.

The experience should be driven heavily by:

- photography
- cinematic video
- visual storytelling
- typography
- composition
- movement
- depth
- interaction
- carefully designed transitions
- meaningful micro-interactions

Copy should support the visuals rather than compete with them.

The design should allow large imagery and typography to create moments of visual breathing room.

Whitespace should be treated as part of the composition.

Motion should have purpose.

When animation, scroll-linked movement, parallax, image sequencing, 3D or WebGL can meaningfully improve the experience, the implementation should consider them intelligently.

Technology should serve the experience.

---

# 7. Glass as an Interface Language

The public experience will use a refined glassmorphism language.

The cinematic footage can contain rich colors, warm golden lighting, wedding reds, cool exterior tones, floral colors and other visual variation.

The interface should therefore provide a calmer visual layer above that footage.

Glass surfaces should feel refined and architectural:

- translucent
- elegant
- tactile
- restrained
- spatial
- readable

The glass interface should create separation between the moving visual world and the interactive layer while still allowing the underlying environment to remain visible.

The overall system should feel cohesive even when the underlying footage changes dramatically.

The interface should feel like it belongs to Amrit Palace rather than feeling like a generic glassmorphism UI kit.

---

# 8. Brand Personality

Amrit Palace should feel:

- welcoming
- elegant
- warm
- celebratory
- trustworthy
- contemporary
- human
- versatile

The experience should balance sophistication with familiarity.

It should feel appropriate for a family arriving for a celebration, a guest staying overnight, a couple hosting a wedding, a child celebrating a birthday and professionals attending a meeting.

The brand should feel aspirational without becoming disconnected from the real property.

---

# 9. Authenticity

Amrit Palace is a real place.

The digital experience should therefore be grounded in the actual property, its spaces, its services and its real hospitality offering.

Real assets should be prioritized whenever available.

Generated or enhanced visual assets can be used to support the experience when appropriate, but the resulting experience should remain believable and connected to the real Amrit Palace.

The website should make the real property look its best through excellent presentation rather than inventing an entirely different property.

---

# 10. Cultural Context

Amrit Palace operates in India, and its celebrations should feel culturally natural.

Indian weddings, family celebrations and other occasions should be represented with authentic Indian visual language, people, clothing, décor and social interactions.

The wedding experience should communicate togetherness, family, celebration, tradition and joy.

Birthday celebrations should be able to feel warm, family-oriented and appropriate for children.

Professional events should feel polished and contemporary while remaining naturally suited to the Indian business environment.

The cultural identity of the place should feel present through the visuals and atmosphere rather than through forced explanations.

---

# 11. One Brand, Multiple Experiences

Although the public website, guest portal, chef portal and admin portal have different purposes, they belong to the same product.

They should therefore share a coherent design system.

However, each experience should be optimized for its users.

### Public

Cinematic, editorial, immersive and emotional.

### Guest

Simple, clear, comfortable and hospitality-focused.

### Chef

Fast, operational and action-oriented.

### Admin

Information-rich, structured and decision-oriented.

The same underlying brand should connect all of them.

---

# 12. The Product Should Feel Alive

This should be treated as an interactive product rather than a static collection of pages.

Interactions should feel deliberate.

Examples of the kind of experience we want include:

- cinematic scroll progression
- smooth section transitions
- image reveals
- depth and parallax
- elegant hover states
- contextual navigation
- animated typography
- meaningful loading transitions
- interactive event exploration
- smooth booking flows
- responsive feedback after actions

The exact techniques should be chosen according to what creates the strongest experience.

---

# 13. Mobile Is Part of the Vision

The experience must be designed intentionally for mobile.

Mobile should not be treated as a reduced desktop version.

The cinematic storytelling, typography, navigation, interactions and content hierarchy should all be thoughtfully adapted to smaller screens.

The goal is for the experience to remain beautiful and usable regardless of device.

---

# 14. The Functional Vision

The visual experience is only one part of the product.

Behind it should eventually exist a real operational system.

The long-term platform should connect:

**Visitor**
→ discovers Amrit Palace

**Guest**
→ books a stay

**Guest**
→ orders food

**Chef**
→ receives and prepares the order

**Guest**
→ sees the order progress

**Charges**
→ accumulate against the guest's stay

**Guest**
→ reviews the combined bill

**Guest**
→ completes payment

At the same time:

**Admin**
→ manages rooms, bookings, guests, orders, events, payments and inventory

**Chef**
→ records ingredient consumption

**Inventory**
→ updates from operational activity

**Admin**
→ can inspect inventory history and operational information

This connection between beautiful frontend experience and real operational functionality is a fundamental part of the product vision.

---

# 16. Prototype First, Production Later

The first major milestone is a polished, functional demonstration of the core experience.

The prototype should prove the most important workflows:

1. Visitor discovers Amrit Palace.
2. Visitor explores a room.
3. Visitor creates a guest account.
4. Guest makes a room booking.
5. Guest explores the food menu.
6. Guest places a food order.
7. Chef receives the order.
8. Chef progresses the order through its workflow.
9. Guest sees the updated order status.
10. Room and food charges contribute to the guest bill.
11. Guest reaches checkout.
12. Razorpay test payment demonstrates the payment flow.
13. Admin manages relevant inventory.
14. Chef records ingredient consumption.
15. Inventory history reflects operational activity.

These workflows should feel real enough to demonstrate the concept convincingly.

The platform can then evolve toward production-grade functionality after the core concept has been validated.

---

# 17. Quality Bar

The quality target is not:

> "A website that works."

The target is:

> **A digital experience that feels deliberately designed, technically thoughtful and worthy of representing Amrit Palace.**

Every major decision should consider:

- Does this improve the experience?
- Does this feel intentional?
- Does this communicate the brand?
- Does this make the product easier to use?
- Does this feel believable for the real business?
- Does the implementation remain maintainable?
- Is there a more elegant solution?

Use engineering judgment and design judgment together.

When there are multiple viable approaches, choose the approach that produces the strongest overall experience while maintaining sound engineering.

---

# 18. Creative Freedom

This document defines the destination, not every road that leads there.

The implementation should use professional creative and engineering judgment.

Explore strong solutions.

Experiment with composition, motion, interaction, typography, spatial relationships and technology when they serve the experience.

The goal is not to mechanically follow a predetermined template.

The goal is to understand the intention behind the product and then build the best possible expression of that intention.

---
